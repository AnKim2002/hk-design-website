Our Value Chain

Process

Vertically Integrated Company

From rough diamond sourcing to the final jewelry piece — we control every step with precision.

1

Rough Diamond Sourcing

H.K. Designs sources rough diamonds from all major international mines connected with the H.K. Group — including De Beers Sightholder, Rio Tinto, Dominion and more. Every diamond is extracted following Best Practice Principles (BPP).

2

Diamond Manufacturing

The diamond-making procedure includes planning, cutting/sawing, bruting, polishing, and final inspection. During planning, a rough diamond is studied and mapped for shape, carat, color and clarity. Each step brings the diamond closer to perfection.

3

Diamond Grading

Diamonds are graded considering the 4C's — Carat, Color, Clarity and Cut. Our diamonds are certified by GIA, GSI, IGI, and HRD.

GIAGSIIGIHRD
4

Jewelry Manufacturing

Our skilled team creates fine-crafted jewelry with modern technology and state-of-the-art machinery. New designs are curated after thorough research to exceed client expectations. We are a proud member of the Responsible Jewelry Council.

5

Diamond & Jewelry Sales

We export to USA, Europe, UK, Australia, Canada, South Africa, UAE, Malaysia, Singapore and more. Visit our international offices in New York, Hong Kong, Shanghai, Antwerp and Dubai.

Quality & Service Guaranteed

H.K. Designs provides stunning jewelry products while guaranteeing the ultimate quality and service with personalized dedicated care.
