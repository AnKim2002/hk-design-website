Our Brands

Portfolio

Strengthening Your Connections

Each brand tells a unique story, crafted with precision and purpose.

Magnificence

Magnify your love

Innovative diamond jewelry with extravagant pieces that truly highlight the size and brilliance of the stone. A breakthrough in the diamond jewelry category — small stones appear significantly larger and more radiant. A quarter carat stone has the beauty of a half carat diamond.

My Diamond Story™

The ring with a brilliant heritage

A remarkable collection of diamond rings that let you set your own love story in stone. Each step in the diamond-finishing and jewelry-making process is captured and documented — from rough diamond to the hand setting.

Seamless

Seamless diamonds for seamless moments

Diamonds that float and shine through without disruptions. Set using patented technology — the bezel's wall thickness is the thinnest in the industry. The visible diamond-to-mounting ratio reaches 85-98%.

Endless Diamond

Solitaire appearance, endless brilliance

A precise arrangement of round invisibly hand-set diamonds with no prongs interrupting the flow. Uses patented Touch Setting technology to create a large diamond look from multiple stones.

Trouvaille

Bridal — A fortunate discovery

Celebrates beauty, adventure and surprises within a diamond. Each Trouvaille Diamond is graded by De Beers Group Industry Services. It celebrates the moment that sparked love between two people.

Arctic Fire

Diamonds of Canadian origin

Certified Canadian Diamonds found in the Northern Canadian territory. All center diamonds are certified Canadian and laser inscribed, clarifying their origin with a diamond quality report and Canadian Certificate of Authenticity.

Line of Love

Straight to the heart

Minimal designs beautifully crafted for an elegant look. Strengthens the connections of love, care and affection. Give Line of Love to strengthen your bond of marriage or connection with family.

Moments of Love

Remember life's sparkling moments

Cheerful, bright designs crafted for a youthful and modern look. A collection of sparkling diamonds that can be added over time to mark every special moment — creating a story you'll never forget.

Milestones

Our Brand Journey

A legacy of trust, expansion, and continuous recognition.

1992
Hari Krishna Exports established in Surat, later expanding to Mumbai.
2002
Established Unit 1; received the first award from GJEPC.
2005
Launched the diamond jewelry brand "KISNA" in India.
2006
Became a direct client of Alrosa.
2008
Built the H.K. Campus manufacturing unit in Surat.
2012
Became a De Beers Sightholder and Rio Tinto Select Diamantaire.
2013
Moved headquarters to The Capital, BKC, Mumbai.
2017
Celebrated 25 years; inaugurated the H.K. Hub by Prime Minister Narendra Modi.
