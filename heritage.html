Our Heritage

Journey

A Legacy of Excellence

From humble beginnings to global prominence — our journey of over three decades.

1992
Established Hari Krishna Exports in Surat & Mumbai. The foundation of a diamond legacy.
1996
Hari Krishna Charitable Trust, Surat — giving back to the community.
2002
Established Unit 1. First award from GJEPC — recognition begins.
2005
Established Unit 2 in Mumbai. Launched "KISNA" Brand in India.
2008
H.K. Campus (Manufacturing Unit) in Surat. Certificate of Export from Govt. of Maharashtra.
2011
My Diamond Story™ launched in New York. Canada Mark Certified Factory.
2012
Certified RJC Member. Became Rio Tinto Select Diamantaire. Became De Beers Sightholder.
2016
Achieved $1 Billion Sales milestone — a landmark achievement.
2017
PM Shri Narendra Modi inaugurated HK Hub. Celebrated 25 Years of Success. 16 GJEPC Awards in 14 years.
2019
Established Unit 7 in Mumbai. JNA Award for Outstanding Enterprise of the Year.
2022
Certified Great Place to Work. Chairman received Padmashri award. Most Preferred Workplace.
2023
Forbes D'Globalist Select Company. Guinness World Record — most diamonds set in one ring (50,907 diamonds).
2024
Certified Great Place to Work — continuing excellence and innovation.

World's top three leading manufacturers of diamond and gemstone studded jewelry.
