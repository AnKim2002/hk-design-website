🌳 Environment

Planted 3.6 Million+ Trees

A Gift to Motherland

When you sow a tree, you don't just sow a sapling but hope. We have sown 3.6 million+ hopes for the future. We have understood it the hard way that trees are immensely essential for our survival. A tree, in its wholeness, can devote each of its parts to sustaining life. From its leaves to its roots and its bark, every part of a tree has something to contribute and has deep value.

💧 Water

Created 164 Lakes & Conserved 36.5 Billion Litres Water

Quenching the Thirst of Communities

Water is such a necessity of life that if not available, life will come to a standstill. In Amreli and Bhavnagar districts in the Saurashtra region of Gujarat, different villages like Rasnal, Badalpur, Chital, Sanosara, Shilana, Harsurpur, and Devlia were facing water scarcity. Through our efforts, we have created around 164 lakes and conserved over 36.5 billion litres of water.

🩸 Health

Blood Donation Drive

Every Drop Counts!

19,276 blood bottles have been collected and donated. We host blood donation drives twice a year to raise awareness about blood donation and its importance in saving lives. "Blood donation is one of the finest gestures one can make to save people's lives," believes Mr. Ghanshyam Dholakia, Founder and Managing Director of HK.

🏥 Medical

Medical Aid

Health is Wealth

Our employees and their health remains of prime importance to us. 9,000 H.K. employees and their family members are provided with free health checkups every year. A blood donation camp is organized to benefit Lok Samarpan Blood Bank in Surat annually. In addition, we have also organized Thalassemia tests for our employees.

📚 Education

Education Aid

Empowering the Next Generation

We believe that nothing should come in between a child and their education. We empower our employees' families with financial assistance for higher education. We also award children who perform exceptionally well in academia or extracurricular activities.

🍽️ Community

Food Aid

Nourishing Body & Soul

Nutritious food is served every day for all the employees at H.K. as well as students. Through this we aim to achieve a sense of community and harmony. By ensuring no one goes hungry, we build a stronger, healthier, and more united workforce.

Together, We Build a Better World

Our CSR initiatives reflect our belief that business success and social impact go hand in hand.
